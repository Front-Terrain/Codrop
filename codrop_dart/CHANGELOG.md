## 1.0.0-rc.1

* Initial release candidate of Codrop for Dart and Flutter.
* High-performance C ABI FFI bindings for `libcodrop`.
* Support for compression profiles: `auto`, `fast`, `balanced`, `compact`.
* Standard Dart `Codec<List<int>, List<int>>` and `Converter` implementations.
* Memory-safe allocation and deallocation handling.
* Cross-platform dynamic library resolver for Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS.
